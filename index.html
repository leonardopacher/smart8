The Smart8 method

From material
to system.

A circular product needs a way to come back. We design the product, the return process, and the measurement plan together.

Map the material

Find where material is lost and who already collects, repairs, or processes it. Establish a baseline before changing the system.

Design for reuse

Prototype a product or service that can be repaired, reused, or disassembled. Test it with the people who will use and maintain it.

Run a local pilot

Set up one collection route, assign responsibilities, and document how the material moves from collection through its next use.

Measure and repeat

Track returns, usable yield, and cost. Use those results to improve the design, decide whether to expand, and prepare evidence for funders.

For NGOs and community partners

Start with what
you already have.

Start with one material, one site, and a team you can realistically support. Co-design the pilot with local staff and participants, then leave behind clear roles, plain-language instructions, and a reporting template.

Ecodesign investment

Back the work that can keep going.

Smart8’s investment focus is ecodesign that can support a repeatable local operation. The first milestone is a pilot with a named operator, a testable budget, and a measurable outcome.

AI can help compare design options, organize field observations, and draft reports. Local teams review those outputs, verify what happens to the materials, and decide what to adopt.
